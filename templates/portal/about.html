About the Portal

The Ayurveda Skill Mapping & Internship Portal is built for SIH 2026 to bridge the gap between Ayurveda education and industry requirements.

Our Mission

Empower BAMS, MD, and PhD Ayurveda students with data-driven skill assessment, personalized learning paths, and intelligent internship matching.

Technology

Built with Django, PostgreSQL, Bootstrap 5, and Chart.js. Features weighted cosine similarity for skill-opportunity matching.
